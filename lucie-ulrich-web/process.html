AI-enhanced workflow

How I work

Problem first. I talk to people, design and test with real users. AI takes the routine. The decisions and the creative part stay with me.

Mindset

AI is a tool. The thinking is mine.

I learned to see a task the way the AI sees it: what it knows, what it's missing, where it starts guessing. Once I gave it the missing context, its drafts started to look like mine.

Then I wrote a set of rules so it thinks more like me. It's a lot of rules. I don't think you can ever put a human mind into text that well. But I'm trying, and experimenting with it every day.
