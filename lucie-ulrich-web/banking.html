Banking from zero

J&T Banka · 2020–2026

I built the design system from zero and the first J&T mobile app. Almost by accident: I was hired to support an external design agency, then the bank saw my potential.

80%

App CSAT, measured in the app from release to 2025

28k

App users: 18k iOS, 10k Android

4.8★

App Store rating, 1.7k ratings

01 · Design system

One system for the whole bank.

I got a logo and a palette. Everything else I built, conservative on purpose: the bank wasn't going to transform for years, and a design system has to last. No wow effects. Function first, and support for the brand.

I looked after the system the whole time, kept it up to date and kept it growing.

+More detail

Retail and corporate. Built in Figma. The iOS and Android apps, web banking and the internal tools all run on it, so clients and bankers see one bank.

02 · Mobile app

The first app for clients who trusted only paper.

We were two designers on the whole app, the whole time.

03 · Testing

WE TESTED EVERY 14 DAYS WITH REAL PRIVATE CLIENTS.

Private clients are a specific group, so random testers wouldn't tell us much. Together with our researcher, I built a panel of real private clients who cared about the product.

04 · Investing

Buying a fund: five screens and a swipe.

Investing in an app was complicated back then, with a lot of regulation. We worked closely with the compliance and legal teams to follow the rules with the fewest compromises for the user.

05 · Prioritization

THE PRESSURE

The list of requirements was so long that we designers couldn't deliver it all in time. So I brought the whole team together, non-designers too, to decide the priorities for the release.

+More detail

Everyone in one room, feature by feature: what does this do for the business? Low score, out. The plan went on a whiteboard in the office.

06 · Feedback

We read every review.

Watchlists, limit orders, price alerts: from reviews straight to the backlog.

07 · Service design

Mapping how the bank works.

A whole-team effort of our Digital Experience team.

+More detail

Interviews and workshops to understand the processes behind the screens.

08 · Outcome

Bankers got time for private clients.

At first they didn't trust the app. Now they bring the ideas for it.

+More detail

The app handles the routine, bankers handle complex advice. Fewer phone calls, a call centre that no longer resets passwords all day.
