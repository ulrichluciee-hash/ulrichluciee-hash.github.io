Portfolio clichés

Not a bot? If you're a human who reads designer portfolios, you've seen these a thousand times. Here they are, explained.

I asked AI for the most common lines in product designer portfolios. Here are the biggest ones from Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT. I'm guilty too, haha. But I tried to say what I do in my own words. It was hard, because I can't share all the details of my projects.

  • “From complexity to clarity”Still stuck somewhere in the middle.
  • “Turning complex problems into simple, delightful experiences”The login screen.
  • “I design products people love”Users love it, says the designer's mum.
  • “Passionate about creating meaningful experiences”A cookie banner.
  • “Bridging the gap between users and business”Business won.
  • “Human-centered designer”As opposed to?
  • “Seamless, intuitive experiences”Onboarding has 11 steps.
  • “Pixel-perfect designer with a strategic mindset”Moved a button 2 px. Strategically.
  • “I wear many hats”My best one: master of fixing the office coffee machine.
  • “I believe good design is invisible”That's why nobody can find the logout button.
  • “Empathy is at the heart of everything I do”Including you, reading this for the 40th time today.
  • “Lifelong learner”Bought a course. Watched one video.
  • “Let's build something amazing together”Something. Amazing. Details later.
