02 · App

Eyes on the train.

Small kids can't focus on a screen for long, and they shouldn't have to. Most of the play happens off screen: they drive the train from memory, with a few big buttons.

Portrait → landscape. Room for big controls in small hands.
All text removed. Nothing to read, nothing to get stuck on.
Simpler controls. Fewer steps, so kids keep watching the train.
Tested with kids. No numbers here. We watched kids play with the app and changed it based on what we saw.
+More detail

The biggest challenge: the users were small children, and there is very little research on designing for them.
