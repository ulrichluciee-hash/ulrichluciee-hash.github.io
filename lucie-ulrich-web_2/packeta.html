SCANNER APP FOR DEPOT WORKERS

Ackee for a logistics client · 2026

A demanding app with a fast click-through flow, used by people with no experience, often on their first shift. I took the original wireframes and decided what the screens really need.

01 · Original wireframe vs. my edits

It has to work fast first.

I saw potential to make the operator workflow more user-friendly. It's an app where speed is everything. Users don't need to know the language, and it may be their first shift.

01
Clear warning states. Every state has its own colour and icon. Green means sorted, red means stop.
02
No language needed, even on the first shift. Icons and one big code do the talking.
03
Clear hierarchy. The destination code is the biggest thing on screen.
04
Sized for the real device. The depot's Zebra scanner, 1:1.
05
Leave any time. Even in the middle of a shift, for a break.
06
Sound confirms every scan. Eyes stay on the parcel, so the work stays fast.
07
Scanning made visible. The main action happens outside the screen, so I drew it into the Figma flow for the other designers.
I also built a design agent for the team after that, with personas that test every flow.

02 · Small idea

A 2-MINUTE VIDEO SOLVES DAYS OF EXPLANATION OF THE PROCESS

The biggest problem was understanding the process. A few minutes of video saved designers and non-technical people hours of trying to understand it.

+More detail

Designers spent days reading the specification and explaining the scanning process to each other. Making the video took a short time. It is schematic, in Czech and Polish, so the same video explained scanning to designers and later to operations and other non-technical people. Everyone talked about the same thing.
